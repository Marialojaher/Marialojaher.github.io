In "In Plato's Cave," by Susan Sontag explains that photographs can change how we understand the world and sometimes affect how we see things. Images can make us feel like we know something even if we were never there. This made me think that a photo can have different meanings. For most people a photo is just an image but for the person who took it it can mean a lot. Everyone can connect t a photo in their own way, which shows that every photograph has meaning.
